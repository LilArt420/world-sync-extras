# 🗺️ World Sync Extras

The playable side of the World Sync server content: the official maps, monsters,
spawns, warps and service NPCs Ragnarok Offline brings over from the iRO/kRO
clients. This mod adds what the server data doesn't have: stories, services and
the official NPCs, block by block.

## 🐍 Jormungandr saga (setting `jormungandr`, on)

- **Episode 20, The Undying One:** the Ancient Ice Canyon, the Lair of the Rgans,
  the Sacred Roots, the Serpent's Maze, a guest in the Ice Castle, the Frozen Zone
  and the Nest of Small Branches, and the memorial dungeon Separated Sanctuary.
- **Episode 21, Age of Heroes:** back in time to Lunaforma and Alberta of old, the
  Georborg resistance, the ghost ship, the cult's temple Gimle and the last battle
  against the giant serpent, with its memorial dungeons.
- **After the stories:** daily quests for both episodes, repeatable memorial
  dungeons, and the traders and enchanters of the Ice Castle and Lunaforma
  (Glacier, Dim Glacier, Georborg and Yorskalf gear).

Full dialogue, the client's own quest log (with an English one for Episode 21,
which the client only has in Korean), the official steps, kill goals and rewards,
with cut-in portraits. No official script exists for these episodes: everything
is written from the kRO client's quest text and navigation tables.

**How to start:** finish Issgard's Episode 19, reach level 200 and talk to
**Horuru** in the adventurers' lodge of the Ice Castle (`icas_in`). You'll need
Rgan Transformation Scrolls (Lunch, on the Frozen Scale Glacier, makes them).
No Episode 19 yet? Turn on `skip_ep19`. Episode 21 starts from **Voglinde** in
the Ice Castle's west wing at level 230. Character variables: `wsj_ep20`,
`wsj_ep20b`, `wsj_ep20c`, `wsj_ep21`, `wsj_ep21b`, `wsj_rg` and a few for the
dailies.

## 🏛️ Varmundt, Amatsu, Juno Library and Chapter 1 (setting `varmundt`, on)

- The Fantasy Book "Dungeon Food" in the Prontera East Library's new wing (level 100+, once a day).
- Varmundt's Chess Garden under the garden pagoda and the Biosphere Deep Abyss research, Rune Crowns
  and enchants (level 250+).
- Seoyeon's Trial under Amatsu (level 70+).
- 🌳 Chapter 1 "Song of the World Tree", from the small ash trees near Prontera and Payon to Destroyed
  Geffen, Verus and the Dark Whisper, with its memorial dungeons and dailies (level 200+).
- 📚 The Juno Library's VR books, through the library annex (level 200+).
- The eroded wing of Varmundt's mansion and the Dimensional Biosphere watchers (level 265+).

## ⭐ Star Forest, Herosria and Ash Village (setting `star_forest`, on)

- The Star Emperor job change in the Meteor Forest: talk to Nalson in Payon as a TaeKwon Master
  (base 99 / job 50).
- 🌸 Sanctuary Herosria's Garden of Dawn, through the portals in the four castle realms: gifts for
  castle lords, Herosria gear, herb jams, the Dimensional Wanderer accessories, refining and enchanting.
- ⚔️ The Herosria siege, Sundays 20:00-22:00 (GMs can start a test battle at the Sanctuary Manager).
- 🌳 Ash Village's shops, enchants and signposts at the edge of the World Tree.

Prices, recipes and rewards that kRO doesn't publish are estimates in both blocks.

## 🧍 Placeholder NPCs (setting `placeholders`, on)

156 official NPCs whose dialog and logic exist in no server data yet, standing
where the client puts them with their own sprite and name. 44 already work
(storage lockers, guild storage, inns, repair, channel switches, the Varmundt
elevator, Jormungandr's contact points, market guides); the rest greet you until
their quests and services are written. Each block of this mod (or another mod
that implements some of them) takes its placeholders over: see
`replaced-placeholders.txt`.

## 👾 World Sync monsters

`db/mob_db.yml` fixes what Divine Pride doesn't publish for 303 World Sync
monsters, until the app ships the same fix: their stats (each the median of the
7 monsters of the same class nearest in level and HP) and their drop chances
(estimated by item type). Temporary.

## 🚪 Entrances (setting `entrances`, off)

34 portals from rAthena's towns and fields into the maps World Sync fills
(Biosphere, Jormungandr, Chapter 2...). The server leaves them out on purpose: a
player whose client lacks those maps would hang on the loading screen. Turn it
on only with recent iRO/kRO client data.

## 🛠️ @npcplace (setting `npc_mover`, GM level 60+)

Stand next to any NPC and type `@npcplace` to move it where you stand, nudge it
a cell, or put it back. Moves are kept across restarts. `@npcplace list` shows
them.

## 📦 Requirements

Ragnarok Offline 1.6.7 or later, renewal, which ships the World Sync content
(maps such as `jor_mbase`, `star_frst`, `ch1_geffen`), and a client with that
data (recent iRO or kRO).

Placeholders and entrances are generated by `tools/world_sync/generate.py
--placeholders` in https://github.com/LilArt420/rathena.
